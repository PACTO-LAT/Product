# Why Stellar

Pacto is built on **Stellar** because it's the most efficient blockchain for **real-world peer-to-peer transactions**, especially in emerging markets.

We’re building for users who:

* Don’t have time to wait 15 minutes for finality
* Can’t afford $5 gas fees
* Need predictable, instant settlement at low cost

Stellar enables all of that — and more.

***

#### Instant Settlement

Stellar transactions confirm in **2–5 seconds**, with finality.\
Perfect for face-to-face deals, mobile experiences, or high-frequency P2P trading.

***

#### Ultra-Low Fees

A typical Stellar transaction costs **less than $0.001** — even when using smart contracts (via Soroban).

That makes escrows affordable even for:

* $5 on-ramp transactions
* Small-merchant trading
* Thin-margin arbitrage

***

#### Native USDC Support

Pacto is designed around **stablecoin liquidity**, and Stellar supports **USDC** natively — with high liquidity and compatibility with major exchanges.

This enables:

* Local merchants to accept and resell stablecoins
* Freelancers to get paid in USDC and off-ramp locally
* Interoperability with other chains and wallets

***

#### Interoperability & Bridging

Stellar is a **hub**, not a silo.

USDC on Stellar can be easily bridged or transferred to:

* Centralized exchanges (e.g. Binance, Coinbase)
* Other chains (e.g. Ethereum, Base, Solana)

Users can **on-ramp on Stellar** and later interact with the broader crypto economy — or just stay on Stellar for cheap, fast payments.

***

#### Optimized for Emerging Markets

Unlike chains built for DeFi speculation, Stellar is built for:

* Global remittances
* Mobile money
* Institutional-grade stablecoin flows
* **Practical real-world usage**

That makes it the **ideal base layer for a decentralized P2P marketplace** like Pacto.
