# Hotel Accepting Stablecoins

A **boutique hotel in Costa Rica** starts accepting USDC for reservations.

* Guests pay in USDC (escrowed via Pacto)
* After check-in, the hotel confirms service delivery
* Escrow releases funds to the hotel
* The hotel earns local currency from guests and resells USDC when needed

✅ Converts Web3 travelers into local liquidity
