# Local Store as On-Ramp

**A small grocery store in Guatemala City** signs up on Pacto, listing their willingness to exchange USDC for cash.

* A customer discovers their listing
* An escrow is created, and the store funds it with USDC
* The buyer visits the store and pays in cash
* The store confirms receipt, and the buyer receives USDC

✅ Enables grassroots onboarding without infrastructure
