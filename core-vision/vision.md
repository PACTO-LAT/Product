# Vision

We believe the next wave of crypto adoption will be **community-led**.

Just like M-Pesa transformed mobile money in Africa, Pacto enables the last mile for stablecoin adoption — allowing:

* Local shops to act as on-ramps
* Hotels and businesses to accept and resell USDC
* Educators and organizers to become liquidity providers

And all of this happens **without custody**, using programmable escrow flows built on Stellar.
