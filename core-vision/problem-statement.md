# Problem Statement

Accessing crypto — especially in emerging markets — is hard:

* Centralized on-ramps are expensive and over-regulated
* Integration is complex and slow
* Informal peer-to-peer (P2P) methods are risky and lack dispute resolution

This limits who can enter the crypto economy.

Pacto solves this by letting **anyone** become a trusted merchant using automated smart escrows — enabling safe, transparent peer-to-peer exchanges.
