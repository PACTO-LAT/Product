# P2P Discovery & Listings

Pacto enables users to discover local or remote peers willing to exchange stablecoins (like USDC) for cash or other payment methods. It works like a decentralized directory of merchants and P2P traders.

There is no centralized database or custodian — discovery happens through open listings where merchants define:

* What they offer (e.g., USDC for cash, or vice versa)
* Where they're located (city, country, or online)
* Pricing and payment methods
* Availability and terms

Users browse these listings and initiate a trade using a trustless escrow flow.

> Pacto does not execute trades — it facilitates secure agreements between peers through transparent, automated contracts.
