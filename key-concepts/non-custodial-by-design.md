# Non-Custodial by Design

Pacto never holds your funds. It doesn’t operate wallets, take custody of tokens, or store user data.

Instead:

* Smart contracts on the Stellar blockchain manage the transaction logic
* Users sign all actions using their own wallet (e.g., Freighter, Albedo)
* Funds are locked in escrow and only released once all conditions are met

This model:

* Increases security
* Reduces legal friction
* Keeps Pacto lean and decentralized

Even in dispute scenarios, resolution is handled by logic encoded in the contract — not by a Pacto admin.
