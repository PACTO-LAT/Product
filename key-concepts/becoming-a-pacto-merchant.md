# Becoming a Pacto Merchant

Anyone can become a merchant in the Pacto network. No licenses, intermediaries, or bank integrations required.

As a merchant, you can:

* Offer to buy or sell USDC
* Set your own prices and terms
* Earn a margin (like on Binance P2P)
* Build a public reputation as a trusted peer

You can be a:

* **Local merchant** (e.g., store, hotel, freelancer)
* **Community organizer** (offering cash-to-crypto to your network)
* **Traveler or remote worker** (off-ramping abroad)

All trades go through a programmable escrow, ensuring that funds are only released once both parties confirm.
