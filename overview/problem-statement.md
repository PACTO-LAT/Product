# Problem Statement

Accessing crypto—especially in emerging markets—is difficult, expensive, and often gated by centralized actors. Traditional on-ramps:

* Require licenses and KYC
* Are slow to integrate and operate
* Charge high fees, making them unviable for microtransactions

Meanwhile, informal P2P channels are:

* Unreliable or opaque
* Lacking in dispute resolution
* Difficult to scale with trust

Pacto solves this by enabling anyone—from a corner store to a hotel—to become a crypto merchant using transparent, automated escrow contracts.
