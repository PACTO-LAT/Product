# Vision

We believe in a world where:

* **Any person or business can become a crypto on/off-ramp**
* **Decentralized, trust-minimized escrows** protect every transaction
* **Community builders and educators** can monetize their crypto efforts

Pacto is inspired by the success of M-Pesa: it enables real utility at the grassroots level. With the power of programmable escrows, we can onboard the next billion users without asking permission.
